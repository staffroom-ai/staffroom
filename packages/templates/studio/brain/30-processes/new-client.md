Before we say yes: can they name the thing they want, and can they decide?
A client with two decision-makers who disagree is a client who takes twice as
long, which is [[10-customers/harlow-and-co]] to the letter.

Then: brief, fixed price, dates, deposit. Nothing starts before the deposit.
