Family bakery on Sydney Road, two shopfronts, opening a third in Coburg.

Marta runs it and makes every decision herself. She replies fast and hates
being chased. Warm and plain is right for them; clever is not.

We did their brand in 2024. They are on the [[20-products/autumn-retainer]] now.

Their morning rush is 6am to 9am, so never ring before ten.
