# Prospect shortlist, Brunswick food

Three worth a call. All independent, all with a shopfront, none with a website
worth keeping.

1. A pasta place on Albion Street, two years old, queue out the door on Fridays.
2. A cheese shop near the station, new owners since January.
3. A bakery in Coburg, which is the street [[10-customers/acme-bakery]] is
   opening on, so mention that or do not call them at all.
