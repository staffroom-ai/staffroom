1. A call, then a one-page brief we write and the client approves.
2. A fixed price, a start date and an end date. Both in writing.
3. Two rounds of changes included. A third is a new quote.
4. Nothing goes live without the client seeing it first.

Invoices are 50% up front, 50% on handover, seven day terms.
