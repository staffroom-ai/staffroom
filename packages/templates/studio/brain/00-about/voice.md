Plain words. Short sentences. Say the thing.

- No exclamation marks. One is a mistake, two is a personality.
- Never "solutions", "leverage", "elevate", "unlock" or "journey".
- Prices are written like prices: $1,200, not "investment starts from".
- Australian spelling. Organise, colour, licence as a noun.
- If a sentence would embarrass you read aloud to a client, cut it.

The test: would Aman say this to someone across a table?
