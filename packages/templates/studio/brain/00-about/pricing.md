Day rate is $1,200 plus GST. Most work is quoted as a fixed price built from
day estimates, not billed hourly.

Typical shapes:

- Brand basics: $4,800. Logo, colours, type, one-page guide.
- Website, up to five pages: $9,600.
- Autumn retainer: $2,400 a month, three months minimum. See
  [[20-products/autumn-retainer]].

We do not discount. If a number is too high we cut scope instead, and say which
part we are cutting.
