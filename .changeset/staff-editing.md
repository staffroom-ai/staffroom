Hire, edit and remove staff and departments from Settings > Staff. Until now the
only way was to open `office/agents.yaml` in an editor — and adding somebody
there did nothing until you restarted the office, which nothing told you. Roster
changes now take effect while you watch, whether they come from Settings or from
editing the file.
