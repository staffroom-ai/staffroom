## What it is

An office you open in a browser. Named staff sit at desks, take a task written in
a sentence, and file the result as a markdown note in a folder you own. Every
provider is yours, every key stays on your machine, and anything that reaches the
outside world stops and asks first.

## Where to start

- **Never opened a terminal?** [Install](/docs/start/install/) walks it from nothing.
- **Want to see it work first?** [Demo mode](/docs/start/demo-mode/) runs with no key.
- **Ready to set it up properly?** [Your first five minutes](/docs/start/first-five-minutes/).

## The part worth reading

[Safety](/docs/safety/) — what your staff may do without asking, what they must
ask about, and what they can never do. It is short, and it is the page that tells
you whether to trust this with your business.
